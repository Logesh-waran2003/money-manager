Privacy Policy for Money Manager

Last updated: January 30, 2026

TL;DR: Money Manager stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information. Your financial data never leaves your phone.

1. Introduction

Money Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. All data is stored locally on your device.

The app processes the following data locally:

  • SMS Messages: Bank transaction messages are read to automatically detect and categorize your expenses
  • Transaction Data: Amount, merchant name, date, and category of transactions
  • Account Information: Bank account names and balances you manually enter
  • Camera Images: QR codes scanned for UPI payments (not stored)

3. How We Use Your Information

All data processing happens locally on your device for:

  • Automatically detecting transactions from bank SMS messages
  • Categorizing expenses and income
  • Generating spending analytics and reports
  • Managing multiple bank accounts
  • Scanning UPI QR codes for payment information

4. Data Storage

Local Storage Only:

  • All transaction data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No cloud backup or sync functionality
  • Data is deleted when you uninstall the app

5. Permissions Required

The app requires the following permissions:

  • READ_SMS: To read bank transaction messages for automatic expense tracking. Only banking-related SMS are processed.
  • CAMERA: To scan UPI QR codes for payment information. Images are not stored.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share any data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, there is no data sharing with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.

7. Third-Party Services

Money Manager does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

8. Data Security

Your data security is ensured by:

  • Local-only storage on your device
  • No internet connection required for core functionality
  • No user accounts or authentication
  • Standard Android security protections

9. Children's Privacy

Money Manager is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the app
  • Delete: Uninstall the app to delete all data
  • Export: Currently not supported (future feature)

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Open Source

Money Manager's source code may be available for review, allowing you to verify our privacy claims.
